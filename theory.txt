A trading system does exactly 3 things
- Accept orders
- Match orders
- Publish results

(everythig else is basically optimizations)

Instrument - (a tradable entity)
Order - (buy, sell)

Bid - Highest price buyers willing to sell
Ask - Lowest price sellers willing to sell

Trade - when a buy meets a sell

Trade { 
 price
 quantity
 buyerid
 sellerid
}

Orderbook* - stores unmatched orders
BUY side (Bids)        SELL side (Asks)
----------------      ----------------
Price  Qty            Price  Qty
100    50             101    30
99     40             102    70

Bids sorted desc
Asks sorted asc

Algo - Orders are matched using a price–time priority algorithm, where incoming orders are continuously matched against the best opposing prices until fully filled or no longer price-compatible.
